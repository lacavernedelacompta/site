5. Cookie policy

Legal basis: Belgian Act of 13 June 2005 on electronic communications (art. 129) — Directive 2002/58/EC ePrivacy — GDPR. Your consent is required before any non-essential cookies are placed.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a site. It allows information about your browsing to be stored. You can change your preferences at any time via the banner at the bottom of the page or your browser settings.

Managing your preferences

You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies via the banner at the bottom of each page. You can also:

  • Disable Google Analytics tracking: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Manage Google advertising preferences: adssettings.google.com
  • Configure your browser to refuse cookies (see your browser's help)
